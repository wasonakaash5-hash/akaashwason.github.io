About Me

The Editor Behind the Cut

I'm Akaash, a video editor based in Punjab, India. I specialize in crafting compelling narratives from raw footage — whether it's a cinematic short film, a brand story, or a social media reel that stops the scroll.

Every project I take on gets my full attention to pacing, color, and audio. I believe a great edit is invisible — the viewer should feel the story, not the cut.
