02 — About

The editor behind the cut

Genius Wason

I'm Genius Wason, a video editor and motion designer based in Punjab, India. I work across short-form social content and longer narrative pieces, with a full post-production pipeline that runs from the assembly cut through color and sound.

My approach starts with pacing — a great edit should be felt, not noticed. Every project gets the same attention to rhythm, tone, and the small details that keep a viewer watching to the end.

Whether it's a fifteen-second ad or a ten-minute brand film, the goal is the same: a cut that serves the story, not the other way around.
